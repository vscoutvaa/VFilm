Join VFilm

Pick your lane.

Choose the account that fits you. Every account type supports the same goal: helping young athletes reach the next level with honest guidance.

Young athlete

9th grade through college players actively pursuing the next level. Build your profile, share your film, get matched with pros.

  • Full recruiting profile (diet, physicals, story, film, academics)
  • AI Mentor Match with verified pros & coaches
  • Game film auto-clipping (coming soon)
  • Ask questions directly to pros
Verification required: school enrollment + roster confirmation. Athletes under 18 require a parent/guardian email on file.

Professional

Active pros, retired pros, and professional coaches. Share your story, mentor the next class, give film breakdowns to young athletes.

  • Public mentor profile (story, accomplishments)
  • Answer questions from athletes and fans
  • Review and critique uploaded film
  • Get matched to athletes who fit your path
Verification required: government ID + league/team roster check + (active pros) social account cross-link.

Fan

Ask verified pros your questions and follow the young athletes you believe in.

  • Submit questions to verified pros
  • Join live Q&A sessions with pros
  • Vote on the questions you want answered
  • Follow athletes and get updates on new film and milestones
Email verification only. Must be 13 or older to create a fan account.
