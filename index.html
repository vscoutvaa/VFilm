Popular questions

Asked by athletes. Answered by pros.

Example questions showing how Q&A will work. Athletes and fans ask, verified pros answer, and the community votes.

Example Young Athlete Asked by D. Patel, 2 days ago
How do I get college coaches to actually watch my film instead of skipping it?
Coach R. Mendoza Pro Verified
Lead with 3 plays that show decision-making, not just dunks. Coaches watch the first 30 seconds, and if it's hero ball they tune out. Show how you make teammates better.
Example Young Athlete Asked by M. Okafor, 5 days ago
Should I commit to a smaller D1 program now or wait for bigger offers?
J. Holloway, Retired NBA Pro Verified
Fit matters more than rank. Programs where you'll actually play minutes develop better than benching at a brand-name school. Ask: who's on the depth chart at your position next year?
Example Young Athlete Asked by S. Tran, 1 week ago
What does a real off-season look like for a college-bound junior?
Coach A. Sterling Pro Verified
4 days/week skill work, 2 days lift, 1 active recovery. Skill work means 60% weakness drills, 40% maintenance. Add film study on Sundays, both yours and the pros'.
Example Fan Asked by Anonymous, 3 days ago
How important is GPA if I want to play overseas right out of high school?
D. Romero, Pro EuroLeague Pro Verified
Less direct for overseas, but transcripts get requested for visas, agent reps, and most prep-to-pro programs. Don't shut doors you'll wish you'd left open.
