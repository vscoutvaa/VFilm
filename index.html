Popular Questions

Asked by Athletes. Answered by Pros.

Top-voted questions from young athletes and fans — answered by verified professionals.

Young Athlete Asked by D. Patel · 2 days ago
How do I get college coaches to actually watch my film instead of skipping it?
Coach R. Mendoza Pro Verified
Lead with 3 plays that show decision-making, not just dunks. Coaches watch the first 30 seconds — if it's hero ball they tune out. Show how you make teammates better.
Young Athlete Asked by M. Okafor · 5 days ago
Should I commit to a smaller D1 program now or wait for bigger offers?
J. Holloway, Retired NBA Pro Verified
Fit > rank. Programs where you'll actually play minutes develop better than benching at a brand-name school. Ask: who's on the depth chart at your position next year?
Young Athlete Asked by S. Tran · 1 week ago
What does a real off-season look like for a college-bound junior?
Coach A. Sterling Pro Verified
4 days/week skill work, 2 days lift, 1 active recovery. Skill work means 60% weakness drills, 40% maintenance. Add film study Sundays — yours and the pros'.
Fan Asked by Anonymous · 3 days ago
How important is GPA if I want to play overseas right out of high school?
D. Romero, Pro EuroLeague Pro Verified
Less direct for overseas, but transcripts get requested for visas, agent reps, and most prep-to-pro programs. Don't shut doors you'll wish you'd left open.
