How the Film Tool Works

Game in. Highlights out.

The same kind of AI clipping tools the pros use — built around finding YOU in the chaos of a real game.

01

Calibrate

Upload 3-5 jersey photos so the AI learns your face, number, and team colors.

02

Upload film

Drop a full game (up to 2GB on the free tier). Processing kicks off automatically.

03

Review clips

AI returns every play you touched. Accept, trim, label, or delete each one.

04

File & share

Approved clips drop into your profile folders. Send to matched pros for critique.

Tech Note

Built on proven sports AI.

Rather than reinvent the wheel, the Film Tool will integrate with established sports-AI services (AWS Rekognition Video, Hudl-class APIs, or specialized partners). That means launch-grade reliability instead of multi-year ML research — and lower cost per clip for athletes.
