About VFilm

The path is real.
So are the mentors.

VFilm is a Vanguard Legacy app focused on helping young athletes reach the professional level with honest feedback, real mentorship, and a clear plan.

My story

Why we built this.

I'm Isaiah Crawford, founder of Vanguard Legacy. I've spent years in locker rooms, film rooms, and long conversations with athletes trying to figure out what comes next.

The athletes who moved forward usually had someone who had been there telling them the truth early. A lot of athletes never get that. I built VFilm to give every young athlete access to that kind of voice.

Too many young athletes are left to figure it out from highlight videos and a recruiting industry that gets paid either way. VFilm connects them with people who have actually walked the path.

What we believe

Vanguard Legacy values

Truth over hype

Honest evaluations and real feedback, even when it is hard to hear.

Pros mentoring the next class

Every pro on VFilm is verified, so athletes know the advice comes from someone who has been there.

Film is the resume

Coaches learn the most from your film. We help you show your full game, not just your best plays.

Path over spotlight

Academics, habits, character, and skill all matter. The pros we talk to say this over and over.

How it works

From sign-up to signed.

Four steps that move you closer to the next level.

01

Build the profile

Story, diet, physicals, academics, on-field metrics, film. Everything a coach needs in one place.

02

Upload film

Get matched

Our AI pairs you with verified pros and coaches whose path matches your goals.

04

Climb the path

Get film critiques, ask questions, and follow a real plan to the level you want.
