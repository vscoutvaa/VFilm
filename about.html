About VFilm

The Path Is Real.
So Are the Mentors.

VFilm is a Vanguard Legacy app built for one thing: helping young athletes get to the professional level with truth, mentorship, and a real plan — not hype.

My Story

Why we built this.

I'm Isaiah Crawford, founder of Vanguard Legacy. Before VFilm, I served as chaplain to all 22 athletic teams at Gardner-Webb University, a Division I program in the Big South Conference. I've spent years in locker rooms, film rooms, and long conversations with athletes trying to figure out what comes next.

What I saw was simple. The athletes who moved forward had someone who had been there telling them the truth early. Most never get that. VFilm exists to put that voice in every young athlete's corner.

Most young athletes never get the truth from someone who's been there. They get parents, hype videos, and a recruiting industry that profits whether they make it or not. VFilm changes that.
